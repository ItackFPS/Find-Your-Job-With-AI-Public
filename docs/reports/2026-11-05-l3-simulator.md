Not met: false activation ≤ 5 % (baseline, SR-1, SR-2 at every ρ); true activation ≥ 80 % within 90 days (0.70, typical). Activation (ACTIVE) must not be enabled on these results.

- **False activation.** The rule is evaluated after every session, so a fixed 0.95 posterior is tested repeatedly over 180 days (optional stopping). At high activity the joint gates are crossed by chance more than 5 % of the time even with independent outcomes, and more under SR-1/SR-2. G3b, G2 and the per-session cap (the levers ADR 0019 allows) reduce it but do not reach 5 %; the G3 diagnostic below shows a stricter threshold alone does not either. A side experiment that evaluated the rule only every 14 or 30 days still exceeded 5 % (16 % at 30 days). Remaining options, untested here and each an ADR 0019 change: a sequential test (an error budget spent across looks), or keeping activation off.
- **True activation.** ACTIVE needs n_LB,min discordant LB positives. At typical activity the LB share of positives (Découverte and date/distance-sorted search) yields only a few within 90 days, so the 0.70 challenger rarely reaches ACTIVE in time. This depends on real LB volumes (AD-6), which do not exist yet.
- Production stays the default in every case (INV-6): failing these criteria keeps the personal ranking in shadow, it never shows an unvalidated ranking.
